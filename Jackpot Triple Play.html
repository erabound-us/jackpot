How to Play

a step-by-step guide to your game

Video Transcript

Jackpot Triple Play from the Florida Lottery offers three sets of numbers for three chances to win jackpots of up to 2 million dollars.

And it only costs $1! How do you play it?

Use the Florida Lottery app to create a digital play slip or fill one out at a Florida Lottery retailer. You can also purchase directly from a Florida Lottery vending machine. First, choose six numbers 1 through 46, or select Quick Pick to let the terminal pick some or all of your numbers at random. Then automatically get two more sets of Quick Pick numbers.

Win by matching three or more numbers on any one line to the winning numbers drawn. Match all six numbers on any one line to the winning numbers drawn to win the jackpot.

Also, you can add COMBO for $1 to combine your matches and win morel

Play Jackpot Triple Play today!

Playing Jackpot Triple Play

  1. Get your play slip

    Visit any Florida Lottery retail location for a paper play slip to choose your numbers. Every Jackpot Triple Play play slip allows you to play one panel manually while two other panels are filled out through Quick Pick. Tickets may be purchased until 10:40 p.m. Eastern Time on the night of the draw. Tickets purchased after 10:40 p.m. will be for the next draw.

    Find a retailer

  2. Mark your panels

    Choose a panel and select six numbers from 1 through 46. You can also mark the QP box (Quick Pick) for your panel and let the terminal pick some or all of your numbers at random.

  3. Choose your add-on

    For an extra chance to win up to $10,000 by combining winning number matches from all three sets of numbers, select the Combo box at the bottom of the panel. Combo costs $1 more per play.

    Save time with Advance Play

    To play the same numbers for more than one draw, select the number of advance draws you wish to play in the Advance Play section at the top of your play slip. If you claim a prize on a winning Advance Play ticket before all of the draws on the ticket have occurred, you will be issued a continuation ticket for the remaining draws with the same play numbers as the original ticket.

  4. Submit your play slip

    Submit your play slip to your Florida Lottery retailer and receive your official printed Jackpot Triple Play ticket with your numbers, draw date(s), and any add-ons. You’ll need this ticket to claim any prizes, so keep it safe!

    Find a retailer

    Did you know you can download and use the Florida Lottery app to play any of our draw games?

    Our app lets you create and save your favorite numbers on digital play slips. Simply have your retailer scan the app and print your paper ticket and you’re good to go. It's that easy! Download on the App Store or Google Play.
