❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Jackpot Analyzer?
A free statistical analysis tool that reads the complete official Florida Lottery draw history and uses AI-driven scoring to suggest lines with the strongest historical patterns. It currently covers Florida Lottery games only: Jackpot Triple Play, Florida Lotto (Lotto X), Fantasy 5, and Cash Pop. It is not affiliated with the Florida Lottery.
Can you add my state's lottery?
We'd love to! Right now Jackpot Analyzer covers Florida Lottery games only, but adding other states is on the roadmap. Every donation directly funds the development of new states and games. If you'd like to see your state included, please consider donating — the more support we get, the faster we can build it.

Can this tool guarantee I'll win?
No. Lottery draws are random. No system, AI, or historical analysis can predict or improve your odds of winning. This tool is for entertainment purposes only.
What is the score number shown on each line?
Each line gets a composite score based on four factors from the full draw history:

• Frequency (up to 40 pts) — how often each number has appeared across all past draws.
• Recency (up to 20 pts) — how recently each number appeared in the last 50 draws.
• Pair co-occurrence (up to 15 pts) — how often pairs of numbers in the line appeared together in the same draw.
• Balance & spread (up to 15 pts) — whether the line has a good mix of low/high numbers and is spread across the full range.

Lines are ranked highest-to-lowest. The top-ranked line is labeled Best. A higher score means stronger historical patterns — it does not mean a higher chance of winning.
How do I use the Generate Lines button?
Click Generate Lines to analyze the latest draw data and get your recommended lines. Each press produces a fresh unique set — previously shown lines are penalized so you never see repeats.
How current is the draw data?
The draw history is sourced directly from the official Florida Lottery PDFs. Data is refreshed automatically on the first line generation of each day, so you always see the most up-to-date results — no action needed on your part.
Are the draw countdowns and Sales Open/Closed badges live?
Yes. Every game panel automatically refreshes its next draw time, countdown, and sales status every 60 seconds in the background — no page reload needed. This keeps the banners accurate across draw boundaries, which is especially important for high-frequency games like Cash Pop (5 draws/day), Fantasy 5 (2 draws/day), and upcoming games like Pick 5, Pick 4, Pick 3, and Pick 2 which also draw multiple times daily.
How does Jackpot Triple Play work?
Each $1 ticket gives you 3 lines of 6 numbers from 1–46. You choose 1 line — the other 2 are always auto Quick Pick. Drawings are every Tuesday & Friday at 11:15 PM ET (Florida local time); sales close at 10:55 PM ET.

The jackpot starts at $250,000 (paid as a 25-year annuity or one-time cash lump sum). When the jackpot pool reaches its cap, any excess rolls down to boost the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th prize tiers.

Add Combo (+$1) to combine matches across all 3 lines for extra fixed prizes up to $10,000.
What are the Jackpot Triple Play odds?
Per line (pari-mutuel game — prizes vary by number of winners):

• 6 of 6 (Jackpot): 1 in 3,122,273
• 5 of 6: 1 in 13,009
• 4 of 6: 1 in 267
• 3 of 6: 1 in 16.14
• Any base prize: 1 in 15.24

Combo add-on (all 3 lines combined): any Combo prize 1 in 5.28; top prize ($10,000) 1 in 323,322.
What is the Combo add-on?
Combo costs +$1 (ticket becomes $2 total). It counts total matches across all three lines combined for a separate fixed prize — you can win both a base game prize and a Combo prize on the same ticket.

• 10+ matches: $10,000  • 9: $500  • 8: $50  • 7: $20
• 6: $10  • 5: $5  • 4: Free $2 Combo Quick Pick

Combo prizes are fixed and are not affected by rolldowns. Duplicate numbers across lines each count as a separate match.
How does Florida Lotto (Lotto X) work?
Each $2 ticket has 1 line of 6 numbers chosen from 1–53. Every ticket automatically gets a random Multiplier (2×, 3×, 4×, 5×, or 10×) that boosts 2nd, 3rd, and 4th prizes. Drawings are every Wednesday & Saturday at 11:15 PM ET (Florida local time); sales close at 10:55 PM ET.

The jackpot rolls until someone wins (no cap). Optional add-ons:
• EZMatch (+$1) — instant win up to $500 at purchase
• Double Play (+$1) — bonus drawing using your same numbers, top prize $250,000 fixed
What are the Florida Lotto (Lotto X) odds?
Per ticket:

• 6 of 6 (Jackpot): 1 in 22,957,480
• 5 of 6: 1 in 81,409 (starting $3,000 × multiplier)
• 4 of 6: 1 in 1,415 (starting $50 × multiplier)
• 3 of 6: 1 in 70.79 (starting $5 × multiplier)
• 2 of 6: 1 in 8.58 (Free Ticket — not multiplied)
• Any prize: 1 in 7.61
How does the Lotto X Multiplier work?
Every Florida Lotto ticket gets one random Multiplier printed on it at purchase: 2×, 3×, 4×, 5×, or 10×. It automatically multiplies the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th prize amounts. The Jackpot (6-of-6) and Free Ticket (2-of-6) are not multiplied.

Example: 5-of-6 starts at $3,000 — with a 10× multiplier that becomes $30,000.

Multiplier odds: 10× is 1 in 26  •  5× is 1 in 8.67  •  2× is most common at 1 in 2.89.
What is EZMatch?
EZMatch is an instant-win add-on (+$1) for Florida Lotto. At purchase, 6 EZMatch numbers are printed on your ticket. If any EZMatch number matches any of your 6 Lotto numbers, you win the corresponding prize instantly — no drawing required.

Prizes range from $2 to $500. Overall EZMatch odds: 1 in 4.71. EZMatch prizes are separate from the main game and Double Play and can be claimed up to 180 days from the first draw date on the ticket.
What is Double Play?
Double Play is a bonus drawing add-on (+$1) for Florida Lotto. It uses your same 6 numbers and Multiplier in a separate drawing held immediately after the main Lotto draw.

Double Play prizes are all fixed (not pari-mutuel):
• 6 of 6: $250,000 (fixed, not multiplied)
• 5 of 6: $3,500 × multiplier (up to $35,000)
• 4 of 6: $60 × multiplier (up to $600)
• 3 of 6: $7 × multiplier (up to $70)
• 2 of 6: Free Ticket

Overall Double Play odds: 1 in 7.61. If you win both a base game prize and a Double Play prize, they are combined into one payment.

How does Cash Pop work and when are the draws?
Cash Pop is a simple pick-and-match game. Pick 1 to 15 numbers from 1–15 and choose a play amount ($1, $2, $5, or $10 per number). One winning number is drawn — if it matches any of yours, you win your play amount × your prize multiplier.

There are 5 draws every day:
• Morning: 7:29 AM ET
• Matinee: 11:29 AM ET
• Afternoon: 2:29 PM ET
• Evening: 6:29 PM ET
• Late Night: 10:29 PM ET
All times are Florida local time (Eastern Time).

Sales close approximately 5 minutes before each draw. You can play multiple consecutive draws in advance.
What is the Prize Multiplier on a Cash Pop ticket?
When you buy a Cash Pop ticket, the terminal randomly assigns a Prize Multiplier (5× to 250×) and prints it on your ticket. This multiplier has no effect on the cost of your ticket — cost is always numbers × play amount.

If you match the winning number, your prize is: play amount × multiplier.

Examples with a $5 play:
• 5× multiplier → $25
• 50× multiplier → $250
• 250× multiplier → $1,250

You know your potential prize before the draw. Higher multipliers are rarer — the Florida Lottery does not publish the exact multiplier odds. Use the Check My Numbers section to calculate your exact prize and estimated take-home.
What timezone are the draw times in?
All times are shown in the local timezone of the state where the game is operated. For all current Florida Lottery games, that means Eastern Time (ET).

Florida observes:
• EST (UTC−5) — early November through mid-March
• EDT (UTC−4) — mid-March through early November

The app always displays the correct current Florida time regardless of where you are viewing it from. Quick conversions from ET:

• Central (CT): ET − 1 hour  (e.g. 11:15 PM ET = 10:15 PM CT)
• Mountain (MT): ET − 2 hours  (e.g. 11:15 PM ET = 9:15 PM MT)
• Pacific (PT): ET − 3 hours  (e.g. 11:15 PM ET = 8:15 PM PT)

If we add games from other states in the future, each game will display times in that state’s own local timezone, clearly labeled on the state row — so you always know exactly which timezone you’re looking at.
