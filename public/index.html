❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Jackpot Analyzer?
A free AI-powered statistical analysis tool that reads the complete official Florida Lottery draw history and uses multi-factor scoring to suggest lines with the strongest historical patterns. It covers all major Florida Lottery games: Jackpot Triple Play, Florida Lotto (Lotto X), Fantasy 5, Cash Pop, Powerball, Mega Millions, Pick 2, Pick 3, Pick 4, and Pick 5.

The scoring engine weighs number frequency across all draws, recency (how recently each number appeared in the last 50 draws), pair co-occurrence (how often your numbers appeared together), and low/high balance and spread across the full number range. Thousands of candidate lines are generated and ranked — you get the statistically strongest set, deduplicated across every press.

Coming features include OCR ticket scanning (point your camera at a ticket to auto-fill Check My Numbers), deeper trend analysis, and multi-state expansion. This tool is not affiliated with the Florida Lottery.

Can this tool guarantee I'll win?
No. Lottery draws are random. No system, AI, or historical analysis can predict or improve your odds of winning. This tool is for entertainment purposes only.
What is the score number shown on each line?
Each line gets a composite score based on four factors from the full draw history:

• Frequency (up to 40 pts) — how often each number has appeared across all past draws.
• Recency (up to 20 pts) — how recently each number appeared in the last 50 draws.
• Pair co-occurrence (up to 15 pts) — how often pairs of numbers in the line appeared together in the same draw.
• Balance & spread (up to 15 pts) — whether the line has a good mix of low/high numbers and is spread across the full range.

Lines are ranked highest-to-lowest. The top-ranked line is labeled Best. A higher score means stronger historical patterns — it does not mean a higher chance of winning.

This keeps the banners accurate across draw boundaries, which is especially important for high-frequency games like Cash Pop (5 draws/day), Fantasy 5 (2 draws/day), and the Pick games (Pick 2, 3, 4, 5) which also draw twice daily.
How does Jackpot Triple Play work?
Each $1 ticket gives you 3 lines of 6 numbers from 1–46. You choose 1 line — the other 2 are always auto Quick Pick. Drawings are every Tuesday & Friday at 11:15 PM ET (Florida local time); sales close at 10:55 PM ET.

The jackpot starts at $250,000 (paid as a 25-year annuity or one-time cash lump sum). When the jackpot pool reaches its cap, any excess rolls down to boost the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th prize tiers.

Add Combo (+$1) to combine matches across all 3 lines for extra fixed prizes up to $10,000.
What are the Jackpot Triple Play odds?
Per line (pari-mutuel game — prizes vary by number of winners):

• 6 of 6 (Jackpot): 1 in 3,122,273
• 5 of 6: 1 in 13,009
• 4 of 6: 1 in 267
• 3 of 6: 1 in 16.14
• Any base prize: 1 in 15.24

Combo add-on (all 3 lines combined): any Combo prize 1 in 5.28; top prize ($10,000) 1 in 323,322.
What is the Combo add-on?
Combo costs +$1 (ticket becomes $2 total). It counts total matches across all three lines combined for a separate fixed prize — you can win both a base game prize and a Combo prize on the same ticket.

• 10+ matches: $10,000  • 9: $500  • 8: $50  • 7: $20
• 6: $10  • 5: $5  • 4: Free $2 Combo Quick Pick

Combo prizes are fixed and are not affected by rolldowns. Duplicate numbers across lines each count as a separate match.
How does Florida Lotto (Lotto X) work?
Each $2 ticket has 1 line of 6 numbers chosen from 1–53. Every ticket automatically gets a random Multiplier (2×, 3×, 4×, 5×, or 10×) that boosts 2nd, 3rd, and 4th prizes. Drawings are every Wednesday & Saturday at 11:15 PM ET (Florida local time); sales close at 10:55 PM ET.

The jackpot rolls until someone wins (no cap). Optional add-ons:
• EZMatch (+$1) — instant win up to $500 at purchase
• Double Play (+$1) — bonus drawing using your same numbers, top prize $250,000 fixed
What are the Florida Lotto (Lotto X) odds?
Per ticket:

• 6 of 6 (Jackpot): 1 in 22,957,480
• 5 of 6: 1 in 81,409 (starting $3,000 × multiplier)
• 4 of 6: 1 in 1,415 (starting $50 × multiplier)
• 3 of 6: 1 in 70.79 (starting $5 × multiplier)
• 2 of 6: 1 in 8.58 (Free Ticket — not multiplied)
• Any prize: 1 in 7.61
How does the Lotto X Multiplier work?
Every Florida Lotto ticket gets one random Multiplier printed on it at purchase: 2×, 3×, 4×, 5×, or 10×. It automatically multiplies the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th prize amounts. The Jackpot (6-of-6) and Free Ticket (2-of-6) are not multiplied.

Example: 5-of-6 starts at $3,000 — with a 10× multiplier that becomes $30,000.

Multiplier odds: 10× is 1 in 26  •  5× is 1 in 8.67  •  2× is most common at 1 in 2.89.
What is EZMatch?
EZMatch is an instant-win add-on (+$1) for Florida Lotto. At purchase, 6 EZMatch numbers are printed on your ticket. If any EZMatch number matches any of your 6 Lotto numbers, you win the corresponding prize instantly — no drawing required.

Prizes range from $2 to $500. Overall EZMatch odds: 1 in 4.71. EZMatch prizes are separate from the main game and Double Play and can be claimed up to 180 days from the first draw date on the ticket.
What is Double Play?
Double Play is a bonus drawing add-on (+$1) for Florida Lotto. It uses your same 6 numbers and Multiplier in a separate drawing held immediately after the main Lotto draw.

Double Play prizes are all fixed (not pari-mutuel):
• 6 of 6: $250,000 (fixed, not multiplied)
• 5 of 6: $3,500 × multiplier (up to $35,000)
• 4 of 6: $60 × multiplier (up to $600)
• 3 of 6: $7 × multiplier (up to $70)
• 2 of 6: Free Ticket

Overall Double Play odds: 1 in 7.61. If you win both a base game prize and a Double Play prize, they are combined into one payment.
How does Powerball work?
Powerball is a multi-state jackpot game. Pick 5 numbers from 1–69 plus 1 Powerball from 1–26 for $2 per play. Draws every Monday, Wednesday & Saturday at ~10:59 PM ET; sales close 10:00 PM ET.

Add-ons: Power Play (+$1) multiplies non-jackpot prizes 2×–10× (Match 5 no PB always becomes $2,000,000 with any Power Play). Double Play (+$1) enters your numbers in a separate drawing with a $10M top prize. Tickets cannot be canceled once printed.
What are the Powerball prizes and odds?
• 5+PB: Jackpot (starts ~$20M) — 1 in 292,201,338
• 5+0: $1,000,000 ($2,000,000 with Power Play) — 1 in 11,688,054
• 4+PB: $50,000 — 1 in 913,129
• 4+0: $100 — 1 in 36,525
• 3+PB: $100 — 1 in 14,494
• 3+0: $7 — 1 in 580
• 2+PB: $7 — 1 in 701
• 1+PB: $4 — 1 in 92
• 0+PB: $4 — 1 in 38
• Any prize: 1 in 24.87

Jackpot paid as 30-year annuity or cash lump sum (~60%). Prizes over $5,000: 24% federal withholding. Florida has no state income tax.
How does Mega Millions work?
Mega Millions is a multi-state jackpot game. Pick 5 numbers from 1–70 plus 1 Mega Ball from 1–25 for $2 per play. Draws every Tuesday & Friday at ~11:00 PM ET; sales close 10:45 PM ET.

Add-on: Megaplier (+$1) multiplies non-jackpot prizes 2×–5× (10× when jackpot under $150M). Match 5 no Mega Ball always becomes $5,000,000 with any Megaplier. Tickets cannot be canceled once printed.
What are the Mega Millions prizes and odds?
• 5+MB: Jackpot (starts ~$20M) — 1 in 302,575,350
• 5+0: $1,000,000 ($5,000,000 with Megaplier) — 1 in 12,607,306
• 4+MB: $10,000 — 1 in 931,001
• 4+0: $500 — 1 in 38,792
• 3+MB: $200 — 1 in 14,547
• 3+0: $10 — 1 in 606
• 2+MB: $10 — 1 in 693
• 1+MB: $4 — 1 in 89
• 0+MB: $2 — 1 in 37
• Any prize: 1 in 24

Jackpot paid as 30-year annuity or cash lump sum (~60%). Prizes over $5,000: 24% federal withholding. Florida has no state income tax.

How does Cash Pop work and when are the draws?
Cash Pop is a simple pick-and-match game. Pick 1 to 15 numbers from 1–15 and choose a play amount ($1, $2, $5, or $10 per number). One winning number is drawn — if it matches any of yours, you win your play amount × your prize multiplier.

There are 5 draws every day:
• Morning: 7:29 AM ET
• Matinee: 11:29 AM ET
• Afternoon: 2:29 PM ET
• Evening: 6:29 PM ET
• Late Night: 10:29 PM ET
All times are Florida local time (Eastern Time).

Sales close approximately 5 minutes before each draw. You can play multiple consecutive draws in advance.
What is the Prize Multiplier on a Cash Pop ticket?
When you buy a Cash Pop ticket, the terminal randomly assigns a Prize Multiplier (5× to 250×) and prints it on your ticket. This multiplier has no effect on the cost of your ticket — cost is always numbers × play amount.

If you match the winning number, your prize is: play amount × multiplier.

Examples with a $5 play:
• 5× multiplier → $25
• 50× multiplier → $250
• 250× multiplier → $1,250

You know your potential prize before the draw. Higher multipliers are rarer — the Florida Lottery does not publish the exact multiplier odds. Use the Check My Numbers section to calculate your exact prize and estimated take-home.
How does Fantasy 5 work?
Each $1 ticket has 1 line of 5 numbers chosen from 1–36. There are two draws every day: Midday at 1:05 PM ET and Evening at 11:15 PM ET (Florida local time). Midday sales close ~12:45 PM ET; Evening sales close 10:55 PM ET.

The jackpot is pari-mutuel (shared among winners) and rolls until won, starting around $200,000. Optional add-on:
• EZMatch (+$1) — instant-win prizes $2–$500 printed on your ticket at purchase; odds 1 in 4.71

Advance Play: up to 30 consecutive days (60 drawings if both draws selected). EZMatch and free Quick Pick prize tickets cannot be canceled.
What are the Fantasy 5 prizes and odds?
Per ticket (pari-mutuel — prizes vary by number of winners):

• 5 of 5 (Jackpot): 1 in 376,992 — pari-mutuel, starts ~$200,000
• 4 of 5: 1 in 2,432 — approx $500
• 3 of 5: 1 in 81.07 — approx $10
• 2 of 5: 1 in 8.39 — Free Quick Pick ticket
• Any prize: 1 in 7.61

Prizes over $5,000 are subject to 24% federal withholding. Florida has no state income tax.
How do the PICK Daily Games work? (Pick 2, 3, 4, 5)
PICK Daily Games are digit-matching games. You pick N digits (0–9) — 2 for Pick 2, 3 for Pick 3, 4 for Pick 4, 5 for Pick 5. There are two draws every day: Midday at 1:05 PM ET and Evening at 11:15 PM ET (Florida local time). Sales close ~12:45 PM ET (Midday) and 10:55 PM ET (Evening).

Play amounts: 50¢ or $1.00 per panel. Play types:
• Straight — exact order match; highest prize
• Box — any order match; lower prize, more ways to win
• Straight/Box — half straight + half box on one ticket
• Combo — covers all straight combinations of your digits (costs more)

Add Fireball to double your ticket cost and get an extra drawn digit that can replace any official drawn digit for more winning combinations.
What are the PICK Daily Games prizes?
Straight prizes at $1.00 play (50¢ play = half):

• Pick 2 Straight: $100  •  Box (2-way): $50
• Pick 3 Straight: $500  •  Box: $80 (6-way) or $160 (3-way)
• Pick 4 Straight: $5,000  •  Box: $200–$1,200 depending on ways
• Pick 5 Straight: $50,000  •  Box: $500–$12,000 depending on ways

Straight odds: Pick 2 = 1 in 100  •  Pick 3 = 1 in 1,000  •  Pick 4 = 1 in 10,000  •  Pick 5 = 1 in 100,000

Prizes over $5,000 (Pick 4 and Pick 5 Straight) are subject to 24% federal withholding. Florida has no state income tax.
What is Fireball and how does it work?
Fireball is an add-on for all PICK Daily Games that doubles your ticket cost. After each PICK drawing, a separate Fireball number (0–9) is drawn. The Fireball number can replace any one of the official drawn digits to create new winning combinations.

Number of new combinations created:
• Pick 2 + Fireball: 2 new combinations
• Pick 3 + Fireball: 3 new combinations
• Pick 4 + Fireball: 4 new combinations
• Pick 5 + Fireball: 5 new combinations

Note: if the Fireball number matches one of the official drawn digits, some combinations may be duplicates, reducing the total unique combinations. You can win both a base game prize and a Fireball prize on the same ticket.

Fireball Straight prizes ($1.00 play): Pick 2 = $50  •  Pick 3 = $200  •  Pick 4 = $1,500  •  Pick 5 = $12,000
What is Box play and how many ways can I win?
Box play wins if your digits match the drawn digits in any order. The number of “ways” depends on how many unique combinations your digits create:

Pick 3 Box:
• 6-way (all 3 digits different, e.g. 1-2-3): prize $80 at $1 play
• 3-way (one pair, e.g. 1-1-2): prize $160 at $1 play

Pick 4 Box:
• 24-way (all different): $200  •  12-way (one pair): $400
• 6-way (two pairs or one triple): $800  •  4-way (triple + unique): $1,200

Pick 5 Box:
• 120-way (all different): $500  •  60-way: $1,000  •  30-way: $2,000
• 20-way: $3,000  •  10-way: $6,000  •  5-way: $12,000

More repeated digits = fewer ways = higher prize per win. Use the Check My Numbers section to calculate your exact prize for any combination.

What timezone are the draw times in?
All times are shown in the local timezone of the state where the game is operated. For all current Florida Lottery games, that means Eastern Time (ET).

Florida observes:
• EST (UTC−5) — early November through mid-March
• EDT (UTC−4) — mid-March through early November

The app always displays the correct current Florida time regardless of where you are viewing it from. Quick conversions from ET:

• Central (CT): ET − 1 hour  (e.g. 11:15 PM ET = 10:15 PM CT)
• Mountain (MT): ET − 2 hours  (e.g. 11:15 PM ET = 9:15 PM MT)
• Pacific (PT): ET − 3 hours  (e.g. 11:15 PM ET = 8:15 PM PT)

If we add games from other states in the future, each game will display times in that state’s own local timezone, clearly labeled on the state row — so you always know exactly which timezone you’re looking at.
